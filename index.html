The Project

What VoyaAI solves

One platform that connects personal travel preparation with institutional duty-of-care — from itinerary design and budget planning to real-time risk awareness and operational documentation.

VoyaAI turns travel requests into structured, itinerary-based plans with hotel, transport, cost and local context.

For organisations, the same workflow becomes a compliant travel and safety process with risk briefing, emergency instructions and approval logic.

Everything is exportable, offline-ready and designed to fit EU hosting and internal operational controls.

Risks

Main risks and challenges

Low product differentiation

Travel planning and risk monitoring are crowded categories; the clear value proposition needs to be highly specific.

Market education

Many buyers still see duty-of-care as a compliance issue rather than a digital workflow opportunity.

Operational complexity

Customer teams have unique policies, local requirements and emergency response workflows that must be integrated carefully.

🛡️

Clear differentiation

VoyaAI stands apart by combining AI-generated travel planning with structured duty-of-care logic in a single, self-hostable workflow. The product is positioned as operational tooling, not generic travel advice.
