Product

Built for modern travel teams and duty-of-care leaders.

VoyaAI brings travel planning, risk monitoring and policy workflows together into one operating system.

Core modules

The system has four operating layers.

🧭

Travel planner

AI generates itineraries, recommends accommodation, highlights transit logic and produces cost estimates.

⚠️

Risk engine

Combines route, regional and event-based insights into destination risk summaries and emergency guidance.

✅

Operations layer

Approvals, escalation rules, manual override controls and policy workflows give teams command and clarity.

Workflow

From travel request to safe arrival.

1. Request intake

User submits destination, date range, purpose and traveller profile. Policy checks are triggered automatically.

2. AI itinerary generation

System suggests itinerary, hotel, transport and budget with contextual constraints from policy and traveller notes.

3. Risk review

Local risk brief, policy exceptions and contingency actions are attached before approval or dispatch.

4. Live monitoring

Trips are tracked via briefings, contact data and alerts while in transit. Dossiers remain accessible offline.
